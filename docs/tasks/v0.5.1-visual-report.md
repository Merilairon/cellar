# v0.5.1 slice B, T12: visual baseline review

Date 2026-11-17. Scope: 32 new Playwright baselines in `tests/e2e/__screenshots__/backup-visual.spec.ts/` and 20 changed baselines (uncommitted working tree). Every new PNG was opened and judged by eye against `docs/tasks/v1.0-ux-spec.md` sections 5.1 to 5.4 and 7. Two of the changed files (`list-search-1280`, `list-search-768`) were also opened; the other 18 are judged from the identical pixel-diff bbox supplied with the task. Playwright, Next and Docker were not run. A pixel scan of the 375 files found no ink clipped at the right edge (the only ink in the last column is table rules and flush-right digits/text).

Summary: no blocking defect. Two themes of Minor defects: (1) the disabled primary button ('Check the file', 'Restore 0 recipes') is hard to read in dark mode and washed out in light; the reason text is clear. (2) The preview info line shows only 'Backup of: ...': the 'From Cellar x.y, exported <date>' part of 5.3 is missing, and the admin 'Owners not found here' line lacks the '(N recipes)' count. Nothing wraps badly, clips or overflows horizontally at 375. The nav shows 'Backup' after Import at 1280 and 768 without wrapping.

Consider a stronger disabled style or a visible outline; the adjacent reason text already carries the meaning (spec 5.3 uses `aria-disabled` with a reason).
2. Minor: spec 5.3 info line 'From Cellar 0.5.1, exported 15 Nov 2026. Backup of: ...' renders only 'Backup of: your recipes' / 'all users' in all preview-* files. Decide whether the version and date are missing from the UI or from the fixture.
3. Minor: preview-admin shows 'marie@example.test' without '(4 recipes)' style count (spec 5.3).
4. Observation: in preview-admin the 'Recipes that already exist' radios appear although nothing conflicts; the spec does not say to hide them.
5. Observation: dark unchecked checkboxes and radios are a grey fill on near-black with a faint rim (about 1.7:1 for the fill); distinguishable from the green selected state but weak. Checkbox rows are about 20 px glyphs with labels; the 44 px target cannot be verified from a still image.
6. Observation (pre-existing, unchanged in the diff): the search submit button beside the search box renders as a small clipped half-disc in list-search-1280/768.
7. Coverage gaps: no baseline for upload progress, 'Restore stopped' partial failure, 'Notes by other users' line, or a keyboard focus ring.
