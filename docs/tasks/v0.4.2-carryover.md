## 3. Security (T12)

Result of the checkpoint: **no High finding.** Fixes made: F1 `Cache-Control: private, no-store` on `copy` and `visibility` responses; F2 the Caddy body limit (section 2, item 1); F3 = C2 (user delete leaves photo files, fixed).

Residuals (not fixed): Q13 no disk quota; Q15 no per-user upload limit; the Next proxy buffers up to 11 MiB of any request body in memory (section 2, item 1); admin reach to photos (item 12); readers copying photos (item 13: a reader's copy takes the photos).

What the negative controls did and did not show: **only the private-note control was run** (removing the visibility predicate makes 3 tests fail). The negative controls for the other authorization and upload checks were **not run**; those tests passing is evidence that the checks work, not that the tests would catch their removal.

## 7. Changelog lines (T16)

- **New endpoints:** `POST/GET /api/recipes/:id/photos`, `PUT /api/recipes/:id/photos/:photoId/cover`, `DELETE /api/photos/:id`, `PUT/DELETE /api/recipes/:id/rating`, `GET/POST /api/recipes/:id/notes`, `PUT/DELETE /api/recipes/:id/notes/:noteId`. `GET /api/photos/:id` gains `?size=thumb`. `GET /api/recipes` accepts `min_rating` and returns `cover_photo_id`, `rating_avg` and `rating_count`. See `docs/data-model.md` section 5.
- **Migration `0007_note_rating_thumb`:** tables `note` and `rating`, column `photo.thumb_path`, FTS table `note_fts` with three triggers. No change to `recipe_fts` or its triggers; `recipe_fts.notes` stays reserved.
- **Search:** note text is searched (shared notes, plus your own private notes); a note-only match ranks after all other matches.
- **Deployment:** proxies must allow request bodies of at least 10 MiB on the photo upload route. The bundled `Caddyfile` does (syntax and matcher validated at the gate; not end to end through the app). `next.config.ts` sets `proxyClientMaxBodySize: '11mb'`.
- **Behavior:** `copy` and `visibility` responses are `no-store`; deleting a user with their recipes also removes their photo files.
- **UI:** photos (upload on the saved recipe page, cover on cards), notes, ratings and a rating filter, cook mode.
- **No new runtime dependency.** `git diff` shows no change to `package.json` or `package-lock.json` in the working tree.
